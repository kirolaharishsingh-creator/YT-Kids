# Idea: The Blue Jackal

**Source:** Panchatantra (see `research/blue-jackal/notes.md`)
**Backlog entry:** #003 in `docs/content-backlog-100.md`

A jackal accidentally dyed blue convinces the forest he's a special ruler
sent to lead them — until his own instincts (howling along with other
jackals at night) give away his true nature and expose the disguise.

**Moral:** You can't hide what you truly are — a borrowed identity never
survives contact with your real instincts.

**Why #3:** distinct tone from #1 (cleverness beats a tyrant) and #2
(mercy/self-sacrifice) — this one is about false identity and
self-exposure. Visually striking (a blue jackal is an immediate,
scroll-stopping image) and single-location simple to produce.
