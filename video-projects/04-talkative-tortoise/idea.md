# Idea: The Talkative Tortoise

**Source:** Panchatantra (see `research/talkative-tortoise/notes.md`)
**Backlog entry:** #004 in `docs/content-backlog-100.md`

A tortoise, warned to stay silent while two geese carry him by a stick
through the air to a new pond, can't resist responding to mocking
villagers below — and loses his grip.

**Moral:** Think before you speak — some things are better left unsaid.

**Why #4:** a different lesson than #1-3 (impulse control / knowing when
to stay quiet), visually distinctive (a flying tortoise held by a stick is
an immediately strange, scroll-stopping image), and a good reminder that
this channel's fables aren't all gentle — this one has a genuinely dark
traditional ending (the fall is usually fatal) that needs careful,
non-graphic handling, same as fable #1's implied-death well scene.
