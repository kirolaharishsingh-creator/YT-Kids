# Idea: The Musical Donkey

**Source:** Panchatantra, Book 5 (see `research/musical-donkey/notes.md`)
**Backlog entry:** #005 in `docs/content-backlog-100.md`

A donkey and a jackal raid farms together at night — until the donkey's
vanity gets the better of him and he insists on singing under the
moonlight, despite the jackal's warnings, waking the farmers and getting
caught.

**Moral:** Even talent has a time and place — vanity invites danger.

**Why #5:** lighter, more comedic tone than #2 or #4 — a fun "vanity gets
you caught" story with a strong, silly visual hook (a donkey serenading
the moon). Good tonal variety after four fables that leaned more serious.
