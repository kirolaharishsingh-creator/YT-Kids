# Idea: The Lion and the Rabbit

**Slug:** `lion-and-the-rabbit`
**Why now:** Pilot video for the channel — establishes the mascot, art style,
and narration voice that every future fable will reuse. Chosen for minimal
cast (2 characters + 1 prop location) to keep the first AI-generation pass
simple to validate.

**Angle:** Classic wit-beats-strength Panchatantra tale, told in 18 seconds
through the recurring peacock narrator.
