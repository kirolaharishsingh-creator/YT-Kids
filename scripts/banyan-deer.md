# Script: The Banyan Deer's Offer

**Format:** 30 seconds · vertical 9:16 · Jataka Tales fable series
(2 mascot shots × 5s + 5 story shots × 4s = 30s, per the shot-list)
**Status:** ✅ fact-checked (see `research/banyan-deer/notes.md`) — ready
for shot-list/production

---

## Beat 1 — Hook (0:00–0:05) — Mascot (Shot 1, 5s)

**Visual:** The peacock narrator mascot, perched on a branch, addresses camera.
**On-screen text:** "The Banyan Deer's Offer 🦌"
**Narration (VO):**
> "Would you die to save a stranger?"

---

## Beat 2 — Setup (0:05–0:09) — In-scene (Shot 2, 4s)

**Visual:** Two deer herds in a forest clearing, a king's hunting party
approaching in the distance. A golden deer (the Banyan Deer) stands apart as
the herds' agreed arrangement is shown — one deer sent each day instead of a
panicked hunt.
**Narration (VO):**
> "The deer struck a deal: one sent to the king each day."

---

## Beat 3 — The Turn (0:09–0:17) — In-scene (Shots 3-4, 4s each)

**Visual:** A pregnant doe pleads before the Branch Deer's leader, who turns
away. Cut to her approaching the golden Banyan Deer instead. He steps
forward without hesitation.
**Narration (VO):**
> "When a mother-to-be was chosen, her leader refused to help. The Banyan
> Deer took her place."

---

## Beat 4 — The Payoff (0:17–0:25) — In-scene (Shots 5-6, 4s each)

**Visual:** The king, raised blade in hand, freezes at the sight of the
golden deer kneeling calmly before him. His expression shifts from
confusion to awe.
**Narration (VO):**
> "The king couldn't kill the noblest deer in the forest."

---

## Beat 5 — Moral (0:25–0:30) — Mascot (Shot 7, 5s)

**Visual:** Back to the peacock mascot, warm expression.
**On-screen text:** "Moral: A true leader gives before he takes."
**Narration (VO):**
> "He spared every animal in the forest. Moral of the story? Mercy
> answered with mercy makes a real leader."

---

## Length note

**Trimmed and retargeted 2026-09-16.** The original narration (78 words)
generated at **59.4 seconds** — an unusually slow pace (~1.3 words/sec)
compared to fable #1's voiceover (~2.3 words/sec for similar phrasing),
possibly triggered by the ellipsis and comma-heavy, pause-inviting
sentence structure in the original draft. Trimmed to ~40 words, removing
the ellipsis and long comma clauses in favor of short, direct sentences.

**Confirmed target: 30 seconds total** — 2 mascot shots × 5s (already
fixed, reused from fable #1) + 5 story shots × 4s = 30s. Beat timecodes
above are sized to this structure. Voiceover not yet regenerated to
confirm the trimmed script's actual duration against this target.

## Production notes

- Every in-scene visual prompt must append the fixed style fragment from
  `character-bible/style-guide.md` (fairy tale illustration style, warm
  jewel-toned palette, no photorealism).
- **Mascot shots (Beats 1 & 5) are fully reused from fable #1 — no new
  generation needed.** The locked "talking" animation clips (generic beak
  movement, not tied to specific words — see
  `video-projects/01-lion-and-the-rabbit/shot-list.md`) work for any
  narration, so this fable's hook/moral audio gets placed under the
  existing footage in editing rather than generating new mascot shots.
- The king's "raised blade" in Beat 4 should read as a frozen, non-graphic
  moment — no violence depicted, the tension is entirely in his expression
  and hesitation, not the weapon.
- **Voice: single-narrator, confirmed channel-wide.** The mascot/storyteller
  voice reads the entire narration, including the doe's, the Branch Deer's,
  and the king's lines as quoted speech within the narration — none of them
  get a distinct voice or lip-sync. The mascot shots aren't lip-synced
  either (generic talking motion, per above) — this replaces the original
  Wan 2.7 lip-sync plan, which hit an unresolved backend issue on fable #1
  (see `video-projects/01-lion-and-the-rabbit/lip-sync-audio.md`).
- Same narrator voice (Isla) as fable #1, reused across every future fable.
