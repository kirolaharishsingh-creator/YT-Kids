# Script: The Banyan Deer's Offer

**Format:** 18 seconds · 16:9 landscape · Jataka Tales fable series
**Status:** draft — pending fact-check against a primary Jataka translation
(see `research/banyan-deer/notes.md`)

---

## Beat 1 — Hook (0:00–0:03) — Mascot

**Visual:** The peacock narrator mascot, perched on a branch, addresses camera.
**On-screen text:** "The Banyan Deer's Offer 🦌"
**Narration (VO):**
> "What would you give up... to save someone who wasn't even yours to save?"

---

## Beat 2 — Setup (0:03–0:07) — In-scene

**Visual:** Two deer herds in a forest clearing, a king's hunting party
approaching in the distance. A golden deer (the Banyan Deer) stands apart as
the herds' agreed arrangement is shown — one deer sent each day instead of a
panicked hunt.
**Narration (VO):**
> "To stop the king's hunters, the deer struck a deal: one of them would be
> sent each day, so the rest could live in peace."

---

## Beat 3 — The Turn (0:07–0:13) — In-scene

**Visual:** A pregnant doe pleads before the Branch Deer's leader, who turns
away. Cut to her approaching the golden Banyan Deer instead. He steps
forward without hesitation.
**Narration (VO):**
> "One day, the lot fell on a mother-to-be. Her own leader refused to help.
> So the Banyan Deer went in her place himself."

---

## Beat 4 — The Payoff (0:13–0:16) — In-scene

**Visual:** The king, raised blade in hand, freezes at the sight of the
golden deer kneeling calmly before him. His expression shifts from
confusion to awe.
**Narration (VO):**
> "The king saw the noblest deer in the forest — dying for a stranger's
> child. He couldn't do it."

---

## Beat 5 — Moral (0:16–0:18) — Mascot

**Visual:** Back to the peacock mascot, warm expression.
**On-screen text:** "Moral: A true leader gives before he takes."
**Narration (VO):**
> "Moral of the story? Mercy, answered with mercy — that's what makes a
> real leader."

---

## Production notes

- Every in-scene visual prompt must append the fixed style fragment from
  `character-bible/style-guide.md` (fairy tale illustration style, warm
  jewel-toned palette, no photorealism).
- Mascot must use the locked-in peacock reference image once generated — do
  not re-describe from text.
- The king's "raised blade" in Beat 4 should read as a frozen, non-graphic
  moment — no violence depicted, the tension is entirely in his expression
  and hesitation, not the weapon.
- **Voice: single-narrator, confirmed channel-wide.** The mascot/storyteller
  voice reads the entire narration, including the doe's, the Branch Deer's,
  and the king's lines as quoted speech within the narration — none of them
  get a distinct voice or lip-sync. Only the mascot shots (1 & 5 here) are
  lip-synced (Wan 2.7).
- Same narrator voice as fable #1, reused across every future fable.
