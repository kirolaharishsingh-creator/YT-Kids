# Script: The Lion and the Rabbit

**Format:** 18 seconds · vertical 9:16 · Panchatantra fable series
**Status:** draft — pending fact-check against a primary Panchatantra
translation (see `research/lion-and-the-rabbit/notes.md`)

---

## Beat 1 — Hook (0:00–0:03) — Mascot

**Visual:** The peacock narrator mascot, perched on a branch, addresses camera.
**On-screen text:** "The Lion & the Rabbit 🐰🦁"
**Narration (VO):**
> "What happens when the forest's biggest bully... picks on the wrong rabbit?"

---

## Beat 2 — Setup (0:03–0:07) — In-scene

**Visual:** A large, arrogant lion in a forest clearing, animals bowing before
him nervously. A small rabbit stands at the front of the group.
**Narration (VO):**
> "Every animal had to send the lion food — or he'd hunt them all himself.
> Today, it was the rabbit's turn."

---

## Beat 3 — The Trick (0:07–0:13) — In-scene

**Visual:** The rabbit walks up to the furious lion, calm and unhurried. Cut
to the rabbit pointing off-screen. Cut to the lion and rabbit approaching the
edge of a deep stone well.
**Narration (VO):**
> "'Forgive me,' said the rabbit. 'Another lion in this forest tried to stop
> me — and said HE rules here now.' The lion roared. 'Show me.'"

---

## Beat 4 — The Payoff (0:13–0:16) — In-scene

**Visual:** Close on the lion looking down into the well, seeing his own
reflection glaring back. He roars at it — then leaps in. Cut to ripples
settling on the water's surface. Silence.
**Narration (VO):**
> "The lion saw his 'rival' — and leapt in to fight him."

---

## Beat 5 — Moral (0:16–0:18) — Mascot

**Visual:** Back to the peacock mascot, warm expression.
**On-screen text:** "Moral: Wit beats strength."
**Narration (VO):**
> "Moral of the story? Cleverness beats a bully every time."

---

## Production notes

- Every in-scene visual prompt must append the fixed style fragment from
  `character-bible/style-guide.md` (flat storybook illustration, warm earthy
  palette, simple shapes).
- Mascot must use the saved character reference once generated — do not
  re-describe from text on each beat.
- The lion's fall is implied (splash/ripples + silence), not shown directly —
  keep it non-violent and clearly family-friendly on screen.
- Same narrator voice across all 5 beats.
