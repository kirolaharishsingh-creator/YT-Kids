# Script: The Talkative Tortoise

**Format:** ~18-20 seconds · vertical 9:16 · Panchatantra fable series
**Status:** ✅ fact-checked (see `research/talkative-tortoise/notes.md`) —
ready for shot-list/production

---

## Beat 1 — Hook (0:00–0:03) — Mascot

**Visual:** The peacock narrator mascot, perched on a branch, addresses camera.
**On-screen text:** "The Talkative Tortoise 🐢"
**Narration (VO):**
> "What happens when you just... can't stop talking?"

---

## Beat 2 — Setup (0:03–0:08) — In-scene

**Visual:** A dried-up, cracked pond. A tortoise and two geese huddle
together, worried. The tortoise gestures excitedly at a stick on the
ground — his idea. The geese look at him warily.
**Narration (VO):**
> "When a drought dried up their pond, two geese offered to fly their
> tortoise friend to safety — if he held a stick between his teeth, and
> stayed silent."

---

## Beat 3 — Turn (0:08–0:12) — In-scene

**Visual:** The tortoise airborne, gripping the middle of a stick in his
mouth, a goose on each end. Below, tiny village figures point up and laugh.
**Narration (VO):**
> "As they soared over a village, people below pointed and laughed at the
> strange sight."

---

## Beat 4 — Payoff (0:12–0:17) — In-scene

**Visual:** Close on the tortoise's face, straining to stay quiet, then
opening his mouth. Cut to the stick falling away, empty air where he was.
**Do not show impact/landing** — cut to a still, quiet sky or ground shot,
implying the fall without depicting it, same non-graphic treatment as
fable #1's well scene.
**Narration (VO):**
> "The tortoise couldn't resist snapping back — and the moment he opened
> his mouth, he lost his grip."

---

## Beat 5 — Moral (0:17–0:20) — Mascot

**Visual:** Back to the peacock mascot, warm expression.
**On-screen text:** "Moral: Think before you speak."
**Narration (VO):**
> "Moral of the story? Some things are better left unsaid."

---

## Production notes

- **Mascot shots (Beats 1 & 5) are fully reused from fable #1 — no new
  generation needed.** Same generic-talking clips as fables #2 and #3
  (`6a35ec14-57c1-4bba-8243-7e8be5f48e61` for the hook,
  `3abdf454-7684-460a-a2d2-7af777ae1fca` for the moral) — this fable's
  hook/moral audio gets placed under the existing footage in editing.
- Every in-scene visual prompt must append the fixed style fragment from
  `character-bible/style-guide.md`, plus the anti-split-screen/anti-panel
  language and individual-pose guidance from
  `video-projects/01-lion-and-the-rabbit/shot-2-iteration-log.md` and
  `shot-3-iteration-log.md`.
- **Content safety, important:** the traditional ending is the tortoise's
  death from the fall. Keep this entirely implied and non-graphic — cut
  away before any impact, use stillness/silence the way fable #1 handled
  the lion's fall into the well. Do not depict the landing or any injury.
- A fable-specific character reference sheet (tortoise + two geese) should
  be generated and locked first, same process as fable #1's
  `character-reference-prompt.md`.
- Location: pond/drought setting for Beat 2, aerial/village setting for
  Beats 3-4 — two distinct locations, more than fable #3's single-location
  approach but similar in scope to fable #1's clearing-to-well transition.
  Use the locked `Neelvan-Forest` Element (id
  `e59c0df0-180e-4d97-9ca1-4ac2a550ef36`) where the setting is still
  forest/pond-adjacent; the aerial village shots will need new
  location-specific prompting since a human village hasn't been part of
  the locked world-bible yet.
- **Voice: single-narrator, confirmed channel-wide.** Same Isla voice as
  every prior fable; no other character gets a distinct voice or lip-sync.
- **Length discipline:** keep narration tight to land close to 18-20s,
  following fable #3's approach rather than fable #1's 33.7s overrun.
