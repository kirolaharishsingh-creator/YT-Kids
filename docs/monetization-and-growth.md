# Monetization & Growth Plan

Numbers below are current as of Sept 2026 (sources at bottom). Revisit this doc
periodically — YouTube changes these thresholds.

## The strategy: vertical Shorts + periodic compilations

This channel produces **vertical 9:16 fables** (~15-20s each) and uses them
two ways:

1. **Individual fables posted as true Shorts** — vertical + ≤3 minutes
   qualifies for YouTube's Shorts shelf, the best discovery path for
   short-form content.
2. **Groups of fables periodically compiled into single longer uploads**
   (same vertical framing, just stitched together past 3 minutes total) —
   since Shorts classification is **duration-based**, not aspect-ratio-based,
   a >3-minute compilation is a regular long-form upload even though it's
   still vertically framed. This is what actually accumulates watch-hours
   toward monetization (see below) — individual Shorts alone don't clear
   that bar efficiently.

This combines the best of both: Shorts-shelf discovery per episode, and a
real path to the watch-hour-based monetization threshold via compilations,
rather than picking one lever and giving up the other.

## Reality check first

Shorts are a **growth/discovery engine, not a direct income source** in the
first several months — expect close to $0 in direct ad revenue from
individual Shorts views regardless of which YPP path you're pursuing. Budget
time/expectations accordingly.

## Two YPP paths, both relevant here

| Path | Subscribers | Watch requirement | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Shorts path** — Tier 1 | 500 | 3M Shorts views (90 days) | Fan funding (Super Thanks, etc.) |
| **Shorts path** — Tier 2 | 1,000 | 10M Shorts views (90 days) | Ad revenue sharing |
| **Standard path** — Tier 1 | 500 | 3,000 watch hours (12 months) | Fan funding |
| **Standard path** — Tier 2 | 1,000 | 4,000 watch hours (12 months) | Ad revenue sharing |

**Change coming:** from Feb 1, 2027, the Shorts path needs **20M views** (up
from 10M) and the standard path needs **8,000 hours** (up from 4,000).
Reaching Tier 2 before that date is meaningfully easier than after.

**Why both paths matter to this channel's strategy:** individual Shorts
build toward the Shorts-views path (10M/90 days) directly. Compilations
build toward the standard watch-hours path (4,000 hours) — and each
compilation counts *much* more efficiently per view than individual Shorts
do, because a 3+ minute video earns real watch-hours per view instead of a
few seconds. **Whichever threshold gets hit first unlocks monetization** —
this channel isn't betting on only one.

## Expected earnings once monetized

- **Shorts RPM** (what you keep): **$0.01–$0.08 per 1,000 views**,
  occasionally up to ~$0.50 in strong niches/geos. A Short with 1M views ≈
  **$30–$100**.
- **Regular/long-form RPM** (applies to compilation uploads): roughly
  **$5–15 per 1,000 views** — dramatically higher per view. A compilation
  with 1M views ≈ **$5,000–$15,000**. This is the real economic case for
  making compilations a deliberate part of the plan, not just a
  monetization-threshold workaround.
- **Avoid the "Made for Kids" designation if at all possible**, on both
  Shorts and compilations. It disables comments, personalization,
  memberships, and Super Chat/Thanks, and cuts RPM to roughly **$1–3**
  (vs the ranges above) because only contextual (non-personalized) ads are
  allowed. This is a strong reason the channel is framed as general/family
  folk-tale content, not toddler-targeted — though note YouTube's system can
  still auto-flag content as kid-directed based on characters/tone/language
  regardless of your setting, so keep tone, narration, and thumbnails aimed
  broadly (family/general audience) rather than toddler-coded.

## Revenue beyond ad share

- Cross-posting the same vertical clips to Instagram Reels / TikTok for
  extra reach and their own creator payouts — no extra production cost
  since they're already vertical.
- Sponsorships once subscriber count and engagement are real.
- Compilations double as a natural sponsorship/pre-roll surface too, since
  longer-form ad inventory (mid-rolls) becomes available past 8 minutes if
  compilations are ever built out that long.

## Realistic path to monetization

- **Post individual Shorts daily** at minimum — the Shorts algorithm
  rewards frequency and consistency heavily.
- **Build compilations periodically** — e.g. weekly or biweekly, bundling
  the last 10-15 fables into one 3-5+ minute upload. This doesn't require
  new production, just re-editing already-generated clips together, so it's
  close to free in incremental effort.
- Fables' natural advantage: **high rewatch value** (parents replay for
  kids), which helps average view duration and completion rate on both
  formats — a strong ranking signal for Shorts distribution, and directly
  valuable for compilation watch-hours too.
- Realistic timeline to hit monetization via *either* path with consistent
  posting: **3–6+ months**, potentially faster than a Shorts-only or
  landscape-only approach would have been, since two thresholds are being
  worked simultaneously. Could be faster with a viral hit, slower if early
  videos underperform — budget for the slower case regardless.

## Action checklist (things only you can do)

- [ ] Create the YouTube channel (channel name, handle, profile art)
- [ ] Set channel default audience setting thoughtfully — do not blanket-mark
      "Made for Kids" unless you specifically intend toddler/YouTube Kids app
      placement (see tradeoff above)
- [ ] Turn on 2-Step Verification on the Google account (required for YPP)
- [ ] Link an AdSense account (needed before ad revenue can ever pay out —
      can be done any time, doesn't require being monetized yet)
- [ ] Post individual Shorts consistently (daily target)
- [ ] Set a compilation cadence (weekly/biweekly) once enough fables exist
      to bundle — track this as its own production step, not an afterthought
- [ ] Track views/subscribers/watch-hours weekly across both formats
- [ ] Apply for YPP as soon as *either* threshold is hit — don't wait for both
