# Monetization & Growth Plan

Numbers below are current as of Sept 2026 (sources at bottom). Revisit this doc
periodically — YouTube changes these thresholds.

## Reality check first

Shorts are a **growth engine, not an income source** in the first several months.
Expect close to $0 in direct ad revenue until well past the eligibility threshold
below. Budget your time/expectations accordingly.

## YouTube Partner Program thresholds

| Tier | Subscribers | Watch requirement | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | 500 | 3M Shorts views (90 days) OR 3,000 watch hours (12 months) | Fan funding (Super Thanks, etc.) |
| Tier 2 | 1,000 | 10M Shorts views (90 days) OR 4,000 watch hours (12 months) | Ad revenue sharing |

**Change coming:** from Feb 1, 2027, new applicants need **20M Shorts views**
(up from 10M) or 8,000 watch hours. Reaching Tier 2 before that date is
meaningfully easier than after — this is a real reason to start now.

Since this channel is Shorts-only for now (15–20s clips, no long-form), the path
to ad revenue runs entirely through the harder 10M-views/90-day route.

## Expected earnings once monetized

- Shorts RPM (what you keep): **$0.01–$0.08 per 1,000 views**, occasionally up
  to ~$0.50 in strong niches/geos.
- A short with 1M views ≈ **$30–$100**.
- **Avoid the "Made for Kids" designation if at all possible.** It disables
  comments, personalization, memberships, and Super Chat/Thanks, and cuts RPM to
  roughly **$1–3** (vs $5–15 for general-audience content) because only
  contextual (non-personalized) ads are allowed. This is a strong reason the
  channel is framed as general/family folk-tale content, not toddler-targeted —
  though note YouTube's system can still auto-flag content as kid-directed based
  on characters/tone/language regardless of your setting, so keep the tone,
  narration, and thumbnails aimed broadly (family/general audience) rather than
  toddler-coded.

## Revenue beyond ad share

Direct ads are the smallest piece for a Shorts-first channel. Larger levers, once
there's an audience:
- Cross-posting the same clips to Instagram Reels / TikTok for extra reach and
  their own creator payouts.
- Sponsorships once subscriber count and engagement are real.
- Eventually a long-form spin-off (compilations, longer retellings) if
  production resources grow — long-form monetizes far better per view.

## Realistic path to 10M Shorts views / 90 days

- Post **daily** at minimum — Shorts algorithm rewards frequency and consistency
  heavily; sporadic posting rarely breaks through.
- Fables' natural advantage: **high rewatch value** (parents replay for kids),
  which helps average view duration and completion rate — both are strong
  ranking signals for Shorts distribution.
- Realistic timeline to hit the monetization threshold with consistent daily
  posting and reasonable performance: **3–6+ months**. Could be faster with a
  viral hit, slower if early videos underperform — budget for the slower case.

## Action checklist (things only you can do)

- [ ] Create the YouTube channel (channel name, handle, profile art)
- [ ] Set channel default audience setting thoughtfully — do not blanket-mark
      "Made for Kids" unless you specifically intend toddler/YouTube Kids app
      placement (see tradeoff above)
- [ ] Turn on 2-Step Verification on the Google account (required for YPP)
- [ ] Link an AdSense account (needed before ad revenue can ever pay out —
      can be done any time, doesn't require being monetized yet)
- [ ] Post consistently (daily target) and track views/subscribers weekly
- [ ] Apply for YPP as soon as thresholds are hit — don't wait
