# Brand Positioning

## What this channel is

Classic Indian folk and moral fables — Panchatantra, Akbar-Birbal, Tenali Raman,
Jataka-style animal tales — retold as fast, punchy 15–20 second videos. Each one
delivers: a hook, a quick story beat, a twist, and a one-line moral.

## What this channel is not

- **Not devotional/religious content.** No deities, no mythology requiring
  theological accuracy or risking cultural offense. Folk tales only — kings,
  animals, clever fools, merchants.
- **Not a toddler/YouTube Kids app channel.** Positioned as general-audience
  family content (like a bedtime-story channel), not preschool edutainment.
  This keeps comments, personalized ads, and normal RPM intact — see
  `monetization-and-growth.md`.
- **Not a generic "did you know" facts channel.** That space is saturated with
  low-effort AI content; the fable format is the differentiator.

## Tone

Warm, quick, a little witty — like a smart older sibling telling you a story in
20 seconds flat. Never preachy; the moral should land because the story lands,
not because it's lectured.

## Naming

No channel name has been finalized yet. When picking one:
- Should read as "storytelling," not "religious" or "baby content."
- Should work as a YouTube handle (short, no special characters).
- Avoid names that lock the channel into one specific fable/character long-term.
